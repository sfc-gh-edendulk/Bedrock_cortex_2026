# Sample Queries for AWS + Snowflake GenAI Workshop

## FOMC Document Queries (Cortex Search)

### Basic Questions
- What did the Fed say about inflation?
- What are the Fed's concerns about the labor market?
- Summarize the key points from the January 2024 FOMC meeting
- What economic indicators did the Fed discuss?

### Policy Questions
- What decisions were made about interest rates?
- How does the Fed view current monetary policy?
- What is the Fed's stance on quantitative tightening?
- When might the Fed consider cutting rates?

### Economic Outlook
- What is the Fed's economic outlook?
- How does the Fed view GDP growth?
- What are the inflation expectations for the coming year?
- How is the housing market affecting Fed policy?

## Revenue Data Queries (Cortex Analyst)

### Basic Metrics
- What was total revenue last month?
- Show total profit for the year
- What are the cost of goods sold?
- Calculate the overall profit margin

### By Region
- Show revenue breakdown by region
- Which region has the highest profit?
- Compare North America vs Europe revenue
- What is the profit margin by region?

### By Product
- List revenue by product line
- Which product has the highest sales?
- Show profit by product category
- What products are underperforming?

### Trends
- Show monthly revenue trend
- How has profit margin changed over time?
- Compare this quarter vs last quarter
- What is the revenue forecast?

### Complex Analysis
- Which region-product combination has the highest margin?
- Show year-over-year growth by region
- Identify seasonal patterns in revenue
- What percentage of revenue comes from each product?

## Combined Queries (Agent Orchestration)

- Given the Fed's economic outlook, how might our revenue be affected?
- If interest rates rise, which regions might see reduced sales?
- Based on inflation expectations, should we adjust pricing?
- How do Fed policy changes correlate with our historical revenue?
